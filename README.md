# Bearing Capacity of Shallow Foundation

## Project Overview

This project presents an Excel-based analysis of the bearing capacity of shallow foundations for different soil conditions.

The study calculates the bearing-capacity factors and determines the ultimate and safe bearing capacities for selected soil types. The effect of foundation width on bearing capacity is also studied using graphical analysis.

## Objectives

- To understand the concept of bearing capacity of shallow foundations.
- To calculate the bearing-capacity factors Nc, Nq and Nγ.
- To calculate ultimate bearing capacity for different soil conditions.
- To calculate safe bearing capacity using a factor of safety.
- To study the variation of bearing capacity with foundation width.
- To use Microsoft Excel for engineering calculations and graphical representation.

## Software Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Word

## Soil Conditions Considered

1. Loose Sand
2. Medium Dense Sand
3. Soft Clay
4. Medium Clay

## Methodology

The ultimate bearing capacity was calculated using:

qu = cNc + γDfNq + 0.5γBNγ

The bearing-capacity factors were calculated based on the soil friction angle.

For the φ = 0° clay condition:

Nc = 5.7

Nq = 1

Nγ = 0

The safe bearing capacity was calculated using a factor of safety of 3:

qsafe = qu / 3

## Results

| Soil Type | Ultimate Bearing Capacity (kN/m²) | Safe Bearing Capacity (kN/m²) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Loose Sand | 652.86 | 217.62 |
| Medium Dense Sand | 1382.52 | 460.84 |
| Soft Clay | 131.00 | 43.67 |
| Medium Clay | 189.00 | 63.00 |

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that the bearing capacity varies significantly with soil conditions. Medium Dense Sand produced the highest ultimate bearing capacity of 1382.52 kN/m² and safe bearing capacity of 460.84 kN/m² among the cases considered. Soft Clay produced the lowest ultimate bearing capacity of 131.00 kN/m² and safe bearing capacity of 43.67 kN/m².

The study demonstrates the use of Excel for performing geotechnical engineering calculations and presenting the results graphically.
